Recruitment Workflow Platform

Smarter recruitment workflows, simpler collaboration.

BenchBoard is a recruitment productivity platform under development. It brings job requirements, resume analysis, application preparation and submission tracking into a connected workspace.

Platform Capabilities

Job Description Management

Capture and organize job requirements.

Resume Analysis

Compare resumes with job requirements.

Application Preparation

Support resume tailoring and outreach.

Submission Tracking

Organize recruitment submissions.

Account Verification Emails

BenchBoard is preparing transactional email delivery for account registration, email verification and password recovery.

Verification codes are intended only for recipients who initiate the relevant account action. These emails are not marketing messages.

Development status: BenchBoard is currently in development and is not publicly available for registration.
